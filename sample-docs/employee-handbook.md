# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to the Company

This handbook contains important information about company policies, procedures, and benefits.

## Working Hours

- Standard hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Lunch break: 1 hour (flexible timing)
- Remote work: Available 2 days per week
- Flexible hours: Core hours 10 AM - 3 PM required

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
- 20 days per year
- Accrued monthly (1.67 days/month)
- Must be requested 2 weeks in advance
- Maximum carryover: 5 days

### Sick Leave
- 10 days per year
- No doctor's note required for 1-2 days
- Doctor's note required for 3+ consecutive days
- Notify manager before 9 AM

### Parental Leave
- 16 weeks paid maternity leave
- 4 weeks paid paternity leave
- Can be taken flexibly within first year

## IT Equipment

### Laptop
- MacBook Pro or equivalent Windows laptop
- Replaced every 3 years
- Must use company VPN when remote

### Software
- All necessary software licenses provided
- Request via IT portal
- Personal software not allowed on company devices

### Security
- Use strong passwords (12+ characters)
- Enable 2FA on all accounts
- Never share credentials
- Report security incidents immediately

## Expense Reimbursement

### Eligible Expenses
- Client meals and entertainment
- Travel (flights, hotels, transportation)
- Professional development courses
- Home office equipment (up to $500/year)

### Process
1. Submit expense report within 30 days
2. Attach receipts
3. Manager approval required
4. Reimbursement within 2 weeks

### Limits
- Meals: $50 per person
- Hotels: $200 per night
- Flights: Economy class only

## Performance Reviews

- Conducted twice yearly (June and December)
- 360-degree feedback
- Goal setting and development plans
- Salary reviews in December

## Professional Development

### Training Budget
- $2,000 per employee per year
- Covers courses, conferences, certifications
- Must be job-related
- Manager approval required

### Conference Attendance
- 1-2 conferences per year
- Company covers registration and travel
- Must share learnings with team

## Code of Conduct

### Expected Behavior
- Treat everyone with respect
- Maintain confidentiality
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report violations

### Prohibited Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination
- Sharing confidential information
- Using company resources for personal gain
- Substance abuse at work

## Emergency Procedures

### Fire Alarm
1. Leave building immediately
2. Use stairs, not elevators
3. Gather at designated assembly point
4. Wait for all-clear from fire marshal

### Medical Emergency
1. Call 911
2. Notify security: (555) 123-4570
3. Trained first aiders available on each floor

### Security Threat
1. Follow instructions from security
2. Lock doors if safe to do so
3. Silence phones
4. Wait for all-clear
